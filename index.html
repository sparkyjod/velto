Specialized Capabilities

Bespoke Hospitality Digital Systems

Websites for dining establishments require a distinct balance: poetic hospitality brand storytelling paired with lightning-fast menu navigation and effortless table reservations.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

Editorial Web Design

Custom visual identities translated into clean digital layouts. No generic templates; every viewport is handcrafted for typography and imagery.

02 / INTERFACE

Live Menu Typography

Legible, accessible, mobile-first food and drink menus. Easy for kitchen teams to modify without breaking formatting, featuring dietary taxonomy and pairings.

03 / CONVERSION

Reservation UX

Seamless table booking journeys. Direct integration with SevenRooms, OpenTable, Resy, or bespoke reservation request flows with instant host notifications.

04 / PERFORMANCE

Mobile Performance

Sub-second initial loads on cellular networks. Over 80% of dining patrons browse menus on mobile devices; speed directly protects table cover reservations.

Engagement Framework

How We Work Together

A structured four-phase delivery model crafted around the real operational calendar of restaurant openings and seasonal menu rotations.

STAGE 01

Culinary Discovery

Reviewing your interior finishes, wine lists, kitchen philosophy, and guest demographics to define your visual direction and content hierarchy.

STAGE 02

Editorial Design

Crafting high-fidelity responsive prototypes. We refine typography, color mood boards, spacing, and image grading before writing production code.

STAGE 03

Engineered Build

Developing clean, accessible, and ultra-fast frontend markup. Configuring reservation links, allergen tags, and mobile navigation states.

STAGE 04

Launch & Handover

Domain verification, analytics setup, Google Business Profile schema linking, and staff training so your team can effortlessly update daily specials.

Independent Digital Designer

Behind the Practice

Focusing on one restaurant at a time.

I am an independent designer and frontend engineer. Unlike traditional agencies that delegate client projects across junior tiers or rely on generic website builders, I personally conceptualize, design, and code every single site under the velto. practice.

My appreciation for dining spaces began with an obsession for editorial menu layouts and restaurant architecture. A restaurant's website should feel like walking into its dining room: warm lighting, curated acoustics, and understated sophistication.

I partner directly with chefs, restaurateurs, head roasters, and bakery founders who care about craft. No bloated overhead, no layers of account managers—just intentional, high-quality digital design.
